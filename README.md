### 👋 Hey, I’m Martí Carmona

Born in 2003, I love figuring things out and making them happen.

I competed at a European level in gaming and reached Spain’s top futsal league before I was 15.  
At 15, I built my first video game and a small site to sell software. That’s when I realized I loved making things people actually use.

In 2020, I started selling software in a Discord server. That side project turned into T1C-$40k ARR, 40k+ engaged users, bootstrapped business, run entirely through community.

I studied Industrial Engineering but dropped out to pursue tech full-time. Since then, I’ve focused on learning tech and building and shipping projects.

Now, I’m building **[Soofte](https://www.soofte.com)** — an all-in-one platform for monetized communities.
